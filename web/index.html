Model scope: Single locus; known parental genotypes; complete penetrance; Mendelian segregation; assumed 1:1 offspring sex ratio. No de novo variation, mosaicism, reduced penetrance, variable expressivity, linkage, or pedigree-based posterior genotype inference. All-birth probabilities average the two sex-specific distributions.

Interpretation: Unaffected carrier denotes heterozygous carriers of an autosomal recessive allele or females heterozygous for an X-linked recessive allele. Risk estimates are per pregnancy, not predictions for a specific child. Educational and research use only; not a substitute for genetic counselling.

Privacy: No patient identifiers are requested; calculations run locally. This interface does not send data to a server or store it in browser storage.
